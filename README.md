# ❓ A GENERAL UNDERSTANDING OF WHO IS BEHIND THIS AND WHAT IT IS

**Pavel Durov** – One of the co-founders of Telegram, who in 2018 decided to launch his own cryptocurrency, TON (the original name in the white paper was GRAM, and this name was _[reinstated](https://t.me/durov/521)_ in 2026). On 9 April 2026, Durov _[posted](https://t.me/durov/482)_ on his Telegram channel that changes were to be made to **TON**, referring to them as **MTONGA**. Essentially, this is a technical update to the blockchain, in line with what was previously outlined in the 2018 whitepaper (fast transactions with low fees). As part of these changes, on 4 May 2026, **Pavel Durov** _[dissolved](https://t.me/durov/501)_ the **TON Foundation** (see below for an explanation of what this is) and assumed full responsibility for everything happening within TON. **Telegram** has become the main validator for **TON**.

**TON FOUNDATION** - The foundation was established in 2023 in Switzerland. It is a non-profit organisation and is funded entirely by donations from the community (PLEASE TAKE THIS FACT INTO ACCOUNT, AS IT WILL PLAY A ROLE LATER) and also acts in the interests of the network. Essentially, the foundation is what will drive the blockchain and the community forward together.

 _[Steve Yuna](https://web.archive.org/web/20241231003316/https://ton.foundation/en)_ was the first president of the foundation. On 15 January 2025, he was replaced by _[Manuel Stotz](https://web.archive.org/web/20250118143533/https://ton.foundation/en)_ to help the organisation enter the US market; six months later, on 20 August 2025, _[Max Crown](https://web.archive.org/web/20250828021637/https://ton.foundation/en)_ took over as president (source: web.archive).

## 💼 CASE I: A DONATION FROM THE _COMMUNITY_

On 7 April 2022, the TON Foundation established _[established](https://t.me/tonblockchain/116)_ an ecosystem reserve managed via a 3-of-5 multi-signature arrangement, which was reportedly intended for:

``TON product integrations``
``community growth``
``the development of partnerships``

On 21 April, the official **TON channel** _[described](https://t.me/gram/393)_ this event as a record-breaking raise of $1 billion and thanked the major holders (whales) who had contributed to TON **`"without asking for anything in return"`**.

***HOW IT ACTUALLY HAPPENED***

19 wallets from a single linked cluster of early miners (Large Giver) directly transferred **420.32 million TON**.
A further 17 linked wallets transferred **106.9 million TON**.
Of these wallets:

15 transferred at least **99.99%** of their funds;
a further two transferred **98.98%** and **98.89%** of their balances respectively.

In addition, another wallet contributed **4 million TON**, having previously received **29.27 million TON** from one of the same early miners.
